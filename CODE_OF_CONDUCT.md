# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Tachyon contributors maintain a professional, welcoming, and technically
rigorous environment. We welcome disagreement about design and evidence while
requiring respect for the people involved.

Expected behavior includes:

- giving and receiving specific, actionable feedback;
- separating technical critique from personal judgment;
- respecting privacy, identity, experience, and accessibility needs;
- acknowledging mistakes and correcting them;
- protecting security reports and confidential user data;
- making space for contributors with different backgrounds and communication
  styles.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory conduct, sexualized attention, deliberate
humiliation, doxxing, retaliation, or sustained disruption are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to repository maintainers. Maintainers will
protect reporter privacy, avoid conflicts of interest, gather relevant context,
and choose a proportionate response ranging from guidance to temporary or
permanent exclusion.

Good-faith technical disagreement is not misconduct. Weaponizing process,
security reports, or review authority against another contributor is.
